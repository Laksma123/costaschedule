How to Use & Guide

Complete operational guide for Costa Smeralda Restaurant Schedule app — 100% offline ready while At Sea.

Add to Home Screen / Installation

Save this app to your phone for instant, offline 1-tap access:

  • Android (Recommended): Install the native CostaSchedule.apk for direct home screen launch and automatic clock alarms.
  • iPhone (Safari): Open in Safari → Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow) → Tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): If opened from web/online link, tap menu (⋮) → Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

Note: If opened directly as a WhatsApp media attachment (content://...), Chrome restricts the "Add to Home screen" option due to browser security policies. Please use the APK or save the file to device storage first.

WhatsApp Schedule Import

  1. Copy the full schedule announcement from your WhatsApp department group.
  2. Open this app and tap the yellow "Paste WhatsApp Schedule" button.
  3. The roster is parsed instantly and your assigned duty will be highlighted with the Costa yellow accent!

Smart Alarm Assistant (3 Timings)

Calculates 3 sequential alarms before duty report time:

  • 1st Alarm: Primary wake up call (default 1 hr before duty).
  • 2nd Alarm: Uniform, grooming & equipment check (+5 min).
  • 3rd Alarm: Departure walk to restaurant outlet (+10 min).

Tap "Alarm" to activate Bedside Nightstand Mode. Overrides screen lock to keep your display awake while resting, and sounds a mobile alarm ringtone when it's time to wake up!

2-Day Retention & 100% Privacy

Automatically retains rosters for 2 days (Today & Yesterday). All data is stored strictly on your device with zero server tracking.
